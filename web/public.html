NEWSpotify links are here
!play <spotify link>

Paste a Spotify track, album, or playlist link. The bot reads the songs from Spotify and plays the best-matching audio. Albums and playlists queue up to 100 tracks.

!play <song>

Search, or paste a YouTube or Spotify link.

!search <query>

Pick from YouTube and Spotify results.

!playlist <url>

Queue a YouTube playlist, or a Spotify album or playlist.

!queue

See what is coming next.

!nowplaying

Open the live progress card.

!previous

Go back to the song before this one.

!loop

Cycle song, queue, and off.

!lyrics

Find lyrics for the current track.
